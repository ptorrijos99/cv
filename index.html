About Me

Predoctoral researcher in the Intelligent Systems and Data Mining (SIMD) group at I3A Institute, with FPU21/01074 grant. My work focuses on federated learning of probabilistic graphical models such as Bayesian networks, developing algorithms that enable learning from distributed data while preserving privacy.
